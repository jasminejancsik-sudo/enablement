# Deliverable Template: Individual New Hire Onboarding Tracker
*Example: [Karlie Chen — Onboarding Tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1rb2HY82xgeAExbIBVEK1Wx7vHRnb4yQT_ddAv_rbqk4/edit). Karlie is a Manager, Sales in NORAM who started 21 Jul 2026. Line manager: Francesco Falcone. REN manager: Michel Tornabene. Saved 2026-10-01.*

**Every new sales hire should get a personal tracker like this one as part of their onboarding plan.** It's a sheet the new hire owns, and they tick off each session as they complete it. It also lets their line manager see how far through onboarding they are.

## Tabs to include
| Tab | What it holds | Who fills it in |
|---|---|---|
| **My Onboarding Plan** | Header (name, title, region, start date, line manager, REN manager), then one row per session: Week, Date, Day, Time, Session Title, Type (E-Learning / Live Session), Trainer, Complete? checkbox | Enablement builds it; the new hire ticks off sessions |
| **Who to Meet / Grab Coffee With** | Names, roles and a done checkbox. Aim to finish in the first month. Covers teammates, reps in other hubs (NY, ATL, SF), Partnerships, AM, Deal Management, Financial Partnerships, RBP, BDRs, Marketing, VP Sales and pod leads | Line manager picks the names; the new hire ticks them off |
| **Monthly Goals** (e.g. "August Goals") | Week by week: Session, Trainer, Scheduler (manager or "You"), Completed, Notes. Covers elevator pitches, call recordings, account list review, pillar check-ins, role plays and call shadowing | Line manager sets the goals; the new hire schedules most of them |
| **Reference: Monthly Vision** | 3–4 focus areas for the month | Line manager |

## Notes for future plans
- **Payments Academy comes too early here as well.** Modules 1–6 run on Days 2–3, alongside the first inductions. That's the same problem as the Q4 2025 plan (see [q4-2025-usa-new-hire-plan-notes.md](q4-2025-usa-new-hire-plan-notes.md)).
- Self-paced e-learning blocks (Payments Academy, Product Foundations, RevWay) are interleaved with live sessions. Each Product Foundation pillar course is followed by a "chat with the NY sales team about the pillar" block. That's a good pattern to keep.
- The tracker includes regional live sessions run by REN and SMEs: Rev Org intro, Highspot/Confluence, Competitive Landscape, Commercial Risk, International Outlook, Deal Management, RAC Plans, AI in Commercial, Underwriting, and Regional BPs/GTM.
- From Month 2 the work moves to practice goals set by the manager (the "Monthly Goals" tab). Targets by Labor Day: 6 role plays and 6 elevator pitches with teammates, 4–5 shadowed calls, and 5 call recordings watched each week.
- The four focus areas in Karlie's vision tab: **(1) Fundamentals**: Payments Academy, Product Academy, RevWay, with quizzes. **(2) Practical**: processes and real-life use cases, using the Q3 Onboarding Repository. **(3) Sales Practice**: role plays, pitch, call recordings, shadowing. **(4) Prospecting**: review the account list, build it out, and start outreach at month end.
