## How the plan is built
- **Structure:** Weeks 1–6 are day-by-day schedules (Day, Date, Start Time, Duration, Topic, Location, Completed?, Goals & Notes, Category, Owner, Related Content, New Hire Notes). Months 2–3 are lighter weekly schedules. A separate SME session tracker covers timing by month.
- **Weekly milestones:** Each week opens with a milestone checklist.

| Week | Milestones |
|---|---|
| 1 | Access to all sales tools · Payments Academy foundation modules 1–10 · RevWay courses 0–1 · All compliance training (Nova) |
| 2 | Payments Academy Product Foundations · First knowledge check with pod lead + enablement · Salesforce, Highspot and LinkedIn Sales Navigator self-paced training · Explain Connect, Move and Protect value props |
| 3 | All Payments Academy modules · 5 more hours of RevWay (the full path takes about 9.5 hrs) |
| 4 | RevWay courses 0–5 · Positioning statement · Start Course 6 (Funneling) |
| 5 | RevWay courses 0–7 · Start Course 8 (Propose) · Pillar Move sales perspectives with Francesco and Jaime |
| 6 | RevWay courses 0–9 · Start Course 10 (Objections) |

- **Recurring rhythm:** Daily morning check-in with Enablement. Daily debrief with Enablement and the pod leader. Friday office hours and knowledge check. Pillar quizzes run as a group on Kahoot, with pod leads joining.
- **Knowledge check cadence (every 2 weeks):**
  - Wk 2: company, tooling, market
  - Wk 4: Move and Manage pillars
  - Wk 6: Boost, RevWay 6–7, Propose role-play
  - Wk 8: Boost, trends and agentic commerce, live prospecting
  - Wk 10: Intelligent Acceptance, Protect, RevWay
- **SME sessions:** Tracked separately, grouped into meeting the team, process, industry, product, pricing, sales practice and voice of the customer.
  - Month 1 (Wk 3–4): team intros (Sales, AM, FP, Partnerships, SE, RevOps), STP, Rules of Engagement and incentive ratings, outreach, AI, What Good Looks Like, team goals
  - Month 2: MBO and path to quota, how quota works, deal desk, SAT, technical integration, Pay to Card and Pay to Bank, FX, role plays, vertical SMEs
  - Month 3: pricing (guidance, take rate, calculator walk-throughs), underwriting ways of working, MAF and questionnaires, marketing, negotiation case studies, issuer outreach
  - Recurring: call recording reviews, role plays, "How We Did It"
- **Repository tab:** A resource library covering AI, What Good Looks Like (calls, outreach), and product and pillar links. Worth mining for the content hub.
