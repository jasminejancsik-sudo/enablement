The 90-day journey

Six phases, each with one job. Day 30, 60 and 90 each end with a clear gate, and selling time grows as structured learning steps back.

Learning load, paced on purpose

Structured hours drop from about 15 a week while AEs are landing to about 5 a week once they're selling. Learning is stacked into two focused weeks, Explore at Day 60 and Propose at Day 90, timed to when each skill is needed.

Stacked moments: Day 60 and Day 90

Instead of dripping topics across months, related sessions are grouped into one focused week right before the skill is certified. Fewer things at once, each at the moment it's needed.

Certifications

Two certifications at the moments that matter most in a Checkout deal: discovery, and the proposal. Both are scored 1–5 per section on a shared rubric so the bar is the same across pods.
