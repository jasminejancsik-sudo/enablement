The 90-day journey

Three 30-working-day blocks, each with one job. Both certifications are done by Day 30, so Days 31–90 go to building pipeline and winning deals. Every session is placed by week and weekday. Cohorts starting on the 1st and the 15th follow the same map.

Filled squares mark the four milestone weeks

Learning load, paced on purpose

Structured learning peaks while AEs are landing and building toward their certifications, then steps back so selling time can grow. After Day 30, learning is concentrated in two focused moments: Follow the Flow (Day 60) and the Deal Room (Day 90).

Structured learning hours per week

Certifications: both by Day 30

Each certification has a build-up of sessions in the weeks before it. Both use a standard persona and a standard case, so every AE is scored the same way. Assessments run as one batch day per cohort, and anyone who misses the bar retakes by Day 35 with supplemental materials.

Day 60 and Day 90: stacked moments

Once certified, AEs learn from their own deals. Day 60 and Day 90 each group the relevant sessions into one focused moment.
