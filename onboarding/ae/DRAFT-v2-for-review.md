## 4. The Day 60 and Day 90 stacks

### Day 60 stack: "Do My Job" workshop (Month 2 · Week 8 · Days 54–60)
A half-day, hands-on workshop. Each partner team takes one of the AE's real deals and shows what *they* do with it, when to bring them in, and what they need from the AE.

| Slot | Team | "Do my job" exercise |
|---|---|---|
| 30 min | Deal Management / Deal Desk | Walk an AE's deal through approval |
| 30 min | Underwriting | Review a real submittal: what gets it approved fast |
| 30 min | Solutions Engineering | Scope an integration for the AE's prospect |
| 30 min | Partnerships + Financial Partnerships | Spot the partner angle in the AE's pipeline |
| 30 min | Account Management | What a good handover looks like after signature |
| 30 min | RevOps / BDR | Pipeline hygiene and BDR collaboration on the AE's territory |

Followed by the **Day 60 review** with the manager: pipeline, activity and ramp check.

> **Question C:** Is the name OK? Alternatives: "Day in the Life", "Deal Partners Workshop", "Who Does What".

### Day 90 stack: "Deal Room" (Month 3 · Week 13 · Days 84–90)
The certifications are done by Day 30, so the Day 90 stack is about **winning a real deal**: payments economics and pricing refresher → negotiation case study → the AE presents their **top live opportunity's full proposal** (pricing included) to the manager and Deal Desk for coaching → Day 90 review and graduation.

> **Question D:** Should the Deal Room be scored like a third certification, or stay as coaching only?
