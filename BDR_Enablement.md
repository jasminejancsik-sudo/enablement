# Check In: BDR Onboarding Programme
*Checkout.com · NORAM Revenue Enablement · Draft v1*

## Programme overview
Check In is a five-week onboarding programme for new BDRs. It is built for people early in their career who have little or no payments knowledge. Each week is named after a step in a payment, and each one builds on the week before: first the company and the role, then payments and the platform, then deeper product and teamwork, then a full simulation of the job. In week 5, BDRs take three certifications. When they pass all three, they **Check Out** of the programme, ramped and ready to cold call, sell on social and run the full BDR role.

| Week | Name | Focus | By the end of the week, the new joiner can... |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Connect** | The company, the team and the BDR role | Explain what Checkout.com does, who's who, and what a BDR is expected to do |
| 2 | **Authorise** | Payments basics, the platform, DEPTH, tools and a day in the life | Explain how a payment works, describe the platform in simple terms and plan a BDR day |
| 3 | **Capture** | Deeper product knowledge, working with AEs and other teams | Qualify a merchant, position Checkout.com against competitors and hand a meeting to an AE |
| 4 | **Settle** | Full BDR simulation and certification practice | Run a structured BDR day from research to booked meeting |
| 5 | **Checkout** | Certifications and graduation | Pass all three certifications and start ramp |

### How the programme runs
- **Formats.** Each session is marked **In person**, **Virtual** or **Self-paced**. Role plays, workshops, simulations and certifications are in person. Product modules and cross-team talks are virtual so that experts in any office can deliver them. Short e-learning and reading are self-paced.
- **Daily rhythm.** Each day starts with a 15-minute stand-up with the cohort. Each week ends with a recap, a short quiz and a checkpoint with the BDR manager.
- **Buddy.** Every new joiner is paired with a tenured BDR for the full five weeks.
- **Glossary.** New joiners keep a payments glossary from day one and add new terms each week.
- **Certification prep runs throughout.** Rubrics are shared in week 2, practice starts in week 3, dress rehearsals happen in week 4 and certifications are in week 5.

**A BDR day (starting guidance, to check against our own call data):**
- **Morning:** account research and planning (30 to 45 minutes), then a call block during the prospect's morning, usually 8 to 10am their time.
- **Midday:** emails, LinkedIn activity and Salesforce updates.
- **Afternoon:** a second call block, usually 4 to 5:30pm in the prospect's time zone.
- **Best days:** Tuesday to Thursday are usually the strongest calling days. Monday and Friday are better for research, sequences and social selling.
- **Calendar:** call blocks are booked in the calendar every day and protected.

**Week 2 checkpoint:** Glossary quiz passed. Salesforce exercise completed. The new joiner has built their own weekly calendar with call blocks.

## Certifications
Each certification is scored against a written rubric. Rubrics are shared in week 2 so that new joiners know what good looks like from the start.

| Certification | What it tests | How it works | Scored by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cold calling** | Opening, getting permission to talk, discovery questions using DEPTH, handling objections and booking the meeting | Live role play with a buyer persona | BDR manager and Enablement |
| **Pitch deck** | Explaining Checkout.com's value for one chosen industry and answering questions | 10-minute pitch followed by Q&A | Panel: BDR leader and an AE |
| **Social selling** | A professional LinkedIn profile, a written outreach sequence and one LinkedIn post | Review of the profile, sequence and post | Enablement and Marketing |

**Pass mark:** to be agreed (suggested 80% on each rubric). Anyone who doesn't pass retakes on the Friday of week 5, after coaching.

## BDR expectations
These are the asks of a BDR once they Check Out. Targets marked *proposed* need sign-off from BDR leadership.

### Outcome targets
| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Explore meetings | **8** (period to confirm) |
| Propose meetings | **6** (period to confirm) |
| Trade metric | To be defined |
| Time to first meeting | Within 2 weeks of Check Out (*proposed*) |

### Daily activity standard (*proposed*)
| Activity | Daily target |
|---|---|
| Call blocks | 2 blocks, at least 3 hours in total |
| Dials | 50 or more |
| Personalised emails | 20 or more |
| LinkedIn touches (connection requests, comments, messages) | 10 or more |
| Salesforce logging | All activity logged the same day |

### Ways of working
- **First month after Check Out:** each BDR is linked to their sellers and multithreads into every target account (*proposed:* at least three contacts per account).
- **Handoffs:** every meeting goes to the AE with a completed handoff note.
- **Salesforce:** activity, contacts and next steps are always up to date.
- **Coaching:** weekly one-to-one with the manager, including a review of at least one recorded call.

### Ramp after Check Out (*proposed*)
| Period | Share of full target |
|---|---|
| Month 1 after Check Out | 50% |
| Month 2 | 75% |
| Month 3 | 100% |

## Ongoing support after Check In
- Weekly call coaching with the BDR manager
- Monthly skills sessions and quarterly product updates, from the NORAM enablement calendar
- 30/60/90 day check-ins against the goals set at graduation
- Recertification when the methodology or the product changes
