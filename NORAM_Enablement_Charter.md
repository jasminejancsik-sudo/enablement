# NORAM Revenue Enablement Charter
*Checkout.com · Revenue Enablement · Draft v1*

## Mission
Accelerate NORAM commercial performance by driving lasting behaviour change. Every commercial team member should be **onboarded quickly, skilled in how we sell, fluent in what we sell, and supported throughout their career** at Checkout.com.

> **Onboard → Ramp → Everboard, powered by Skills, Product, and Foundations.**

## Scope
**Who we serve:** NORAM Sales (AE), BDR, Account Management, Solutions Engineering and Commercial Leaders, working with Deal Management, Risk/Underwriting, Strategy and Product.
**Our role:** Regional activation of Global Revenue Enablement. We localise, deliver and reinforce global programmes, and we build for NORAM-specific needs.
**Out of scope:** Sales operations and territory planning, product marketing messaging ownership, HR-led compliance training.

## Pillars

| Pillar | Purpose | What's included | How we measure it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Onboard, Ramp & Everboard** | New hires get productive quickly; tenured team members keep growing | New-hire onboarding (structure, payments, tools, sales process, NORAM context) · role-based ramp pathways with 30/60/90 milestones · everboarding: tenure-based learning paths, recertification, role transitions (e.g. BDR→AE, new managers), change refreshers | Time to first meeting and first deal · ramp completion · recertification rate · promotion readiness · retention |
| **2. | **3. Know Checkout** | Commercial teams can explain and position what Checkout offers | Payments/Product Academy (Acquiring, Issuing, Schemes, APMs) · product refreshers · launch readiness · value messaging and industry use cases | Academy completion · quiz scores · launch readiness · fewer escalations to SE and Product |
| **4. Enablement Foundations** | The right content is easy to find, and people can learn on their own | Content quality and governance · Highspot→Drive transition · Glean adoption · LMS best practices · e-learning and video · commercial AI training and tools · enablement communications (newsletter, Slack, "what's new") | Content usage and search success · fewer stale assets · Glean and AI adoption · self-serve completion |

## How We Work
- **Strategic partnering:** Seat in NORAM leadership and pipeline reviews. We use data to spot productivity bottlenecks before they affect revenue.
- **Intake and prioritisation:** One intake route for requests. Requests are prioritised by revenue impact, audience size and urgency.
- **Regional enablement calendar:** Published quarterly, with recurring everboarding slots (monthly skills sessions, quarterly product updates).
- **Flagship moments:** NORAM delivery for Kick Off, President's Club and Commercial Leadership Summits.
- **Measurement:** Salesforce dashboards connect enablement activity to outcomes. Quarterly impact report to NORAM leadership.
