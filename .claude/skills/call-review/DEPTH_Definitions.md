# DEPTH Definitions — Spoken Script

*Approx. 3 minutes | Audience: NORAM commercial team | Use as a live talk track*

## Opening

“DEPTH is our sales methodology and the way we track an opportunity’s progress: **Discover, Explore, Propose, Trade, and Handover**. The stages help us stay focused on the Merchant’s needs and move the conversation forward in a deliberate, value-led way. They’re not just Salesforce labels: each stage has a different purpose.”

## D — Discover

“**Discover** is the research and planning we do before our first meeting with the customer—or, for an existing Merchant, before the first meeting about a particular issue. We build a useful picture of the company, the people involved, and the market around them. We also plan our opening, what we want to learn, and the next commitment we hope to secure. Good preparation helps us make the first conversation relevant.”

## E — Explore

“**Explore** is where we make a strong first impression, listen actively, and understand what matters to the Merchant. We ask thoughtful questions, follow the areas that matter most, and funnel the conversation to build a deeper understanding of their goals, needs, and challenges. We may need more than one meeting. The point is to understand the Merchant before jumping to our solution; qualification information such as MEDDIC supports that understanding, but shouldn’t be the first thing we lead with.”

## P — Propose

“**Propose** is where we bring forward solutions that address the needs and priorities we agreed in Explore. We connect the Merchant’s goals to specific Checkout capabilities, and support our recommendations with proof points and data—including the value or financial impact where we can quantify it. The Merchant should be able to see the link: ‘You told us this matters; here’s what we recommend and the outcome it can deliver.’”

## T — Trade

“**Trade** is where we discuss pricing and agree commercial terms for a solution the Merchant has accepted as meeting their needs. We present pricing in a conversation, explain it confidently and clearly, handle objections respectfully, and work toward an agreement that benefits both sides. We avoid giving something away without seeking an appropriate commitment in return.”

## H — Handover

“**Handover** is how we deliver on the commitments we made. We keep the Merchant informed, communicate clearly about the actions we need from them, and coordinate internally so the experience matches what we promised. For a Sales Rep, this also means working with the Account Manager on an account plan so ongoing support and account management are set up.”

## Close

“Put simply: **Discover** prepares us; **Explore** helps us understand; **Propose** shows how we can help; **Trade** aligns the commercials; and **Handover** turns our commitments into delivery. The stages work together to keep the Merchant’s needs at the center from the first conversation through implementation and ongoing support.”
